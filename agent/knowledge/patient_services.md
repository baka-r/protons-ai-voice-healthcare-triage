## Do you have wheelchair access?
Yes. All entrances have ramps, and wheelchairs are available at the main reception.

## Is there a prayer room?
Yes. There is a prayer room on the first floor, open at all hours.

## Is there an ATM in the hospital?
There is an ATM in the main lobby.

## Do you offer interpreter services?
Interpreters for several languages can be arranged. Please tell reception when you book your appointment.

## Is smoking allowed at the hospital?
No. The hospital is a smoke-free site, including the car park.

## Can I take photos or record video inside the hospital?
Photography and video recording are not allowed in clinical areas, to protect patient privacy.

## What happens to lost property?
Lost property is kept at the main reception for thirty days.

## How do I request copies of my medical records?
You can request copies from the medical records office on the first floor with your ID card. Processing normally takes three working days.

## What are the public holiday hours?
On public holidays, outpatient clinics are closed, while the emergency department stays open all day.
