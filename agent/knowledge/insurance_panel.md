## Which insurance companies do you work with?
We work with most major health insurance providers. Please bring your insurance card, and the billing desk will confirm your cover.

## Do you offer cashless treatment?
Cashless treatment is available for approved insurance providers. Approval is arranged by the billing desk before planned admission.

## How do I make an insurance claim?
Ask the billing desk for your itemised bill and discharge summary, which you will need to send to your insurer.

## What if my insurance does not cover everything?
The billing desk will explain what is covered and what you would need to pay before treatment starts.
