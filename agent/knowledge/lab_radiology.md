## What tests does the laboratory offer?
The laboratory offers routine blood tests, urine tests, and many other common tests. Reception can confirm whether a particular test is available.

## What radiology services do you have?
Radiology offers X ray, ultrasound, CT scan and MRI. It is on the ground floor.

## Do I need an appointment for an X ray or ultrasound?
X rays are walk in during opening hours. Ultrasound, CT and MRI need an appointment and a request from a doctor.

## Do I need to fast before a test?
Only if you have been told to. Please follow the instructions given to you by your doctor or the laboratory.

## When will my reports be ready?
Most routine laboratory reports are ready the same day. Scans may take longer. Reception can tell you the expected time for your test.

## Do you offer home sample collection?
Yes, home sample collection can be arranged by calling the laboratory during opening hours.

## How can I get my reports?
Collect them from the laboratory counter with your ID card and receipt, or ask reception about receiving them by email.
