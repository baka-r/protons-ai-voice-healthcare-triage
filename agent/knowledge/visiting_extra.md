## What are the maternity ward visiting hours?
Maternity ward visiting hours are from 5 PM to 7 PM, and only one visitor is allowed at a time.

## What are the rules for visiting intensive care?
Intensive care visiting is limited to close family for fifteen minutes at a time. The nurse in charge will guide you.

## What is the policy on flowers and gifts?
Fresh flowers are not allowed on the wards. Sealed gifts are welcome.

## Can I bring food for a patient?
Outside food may be brought for a patient only after checking with the ward nurse.

## Can I stay overnight with a patient?
Overnight stays are limited to one attendant per patient, and need approval from the ward nurse.
