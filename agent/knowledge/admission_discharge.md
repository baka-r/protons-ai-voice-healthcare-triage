## How do I get admitted to the hospital?
Admission is arranged by the treating doctor. Reception will then complete the paperwork with you. Please bring your national ID card and insurance card if you have one.

## What are the room types?
We have general ward beds, semi private rooms and private rooms. Reception can tell you about availability and charges.

## Can a family member stay with a patient?
One attendant per patient may stay, with approval from the ward nurse.

## What time is discharge?
Discharge is usually completed by noon, once the doctor has approved it and the bill is settled. Please allow a few hours for paperwork.

## What do I receive when I am discharged?
You receive a discharge summary, your reports, and the follow up instructions from your doctor.

## Can I get a copy of my discharge summary later?
Yes. The medical records office on the first floor can provide copies with your ID card.
