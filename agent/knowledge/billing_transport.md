## Can I get an itemised bill?
Yes. The billing desk can provide an itemised bill on request.

## Do you offer payment plans?
Please speak with the billing desk about payment options for your situation.

## How do I get a receipt?
Receipts are issued at the billing desk and can be sent by email on request.

## Where is the billing desk?
The billing desk is on the ground floor, near the main reception.

## Is there a shuttle service?
A free shuttle runs between the main gate and the outpatient building every ten minutes.

## Is there a taxi stand?
Yes. A taxi stand is at the main gate.

## Which entrance should I use?
Outpatients use the main entrance. The emergency entrance is on the east side of the building.
