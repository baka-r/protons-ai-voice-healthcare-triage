## Which departments do you have?
We have Cardiology, General Medicine, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Dermatology, ENT and Ophthalmology.

## Do you have a cafeteria and wifi?
A cafeteria on the ground floor is open from 8 AM to 8 PM. Free guest wifi is available in all waiting areas.

## Do you have an in-house pharmacy and laboratory?
Yes. Both the pharmacy and the laboratory are on the ground floor, near the main reception.
