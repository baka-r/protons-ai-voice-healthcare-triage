## What does the Cardiology department do?
Cardiology looks after heart and blood pressure related concerns. It offers consultations, ECG, echo and stress testing. The clinic is on the second floor.

## What does the General Medicine department do?
General Medicine is the first stop for most adult health concerns such as fever, stomach upset, cough, tiredness and long term conditions. It is on the ground floor.

## What does the Orthopedics department do?
Orthopedics looks after bones, joints, muscles, fractures and sports injuries. It has a plaster room and a physiotherapy unit. It is on the first floor.

## What does the Pediatrics department do?
Pediatrics looks after babies, children and teenagers, including routine check ups and vaccinations. It is on the ground floor in the child friendly wing.

## What does the Gynecology department do?
Gynecology and obstetrics looks after women's health, pregnancy care and delivery. The antenatal clinic and maternity ward are on the third floor.

## What does the Dermatology department do?
Dermatology looks after skin, hair and nail concerns. It is on the first floor.

## What does the ENT department do?
ENT looks after ear, nose and throat concerns, including hearing tests. It is on the first floor.

## What does the Ophthalmology department do?
Ophthalmology looks after eye concerns and offers eye tests, glasses prescriptions and minor eye procedures. It is on the first floor.

## Which floor is each department on?
General Medicine and Pediatrics are on the ground floor. Orthopedics, Dermatology, ENT and Ophthalmology are on the first floor. Cardiology is on the second floor, and Gynecology and the maternity ward are on the third floor.

## Do you have a dental, kidney or cancer department?
Our departments are Cardiology, General Medicine, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Dermatology, ENT and Ophthalmology. For other specialities, reception can tell you about referral options.
