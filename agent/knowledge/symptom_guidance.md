## I feel sick and I am vomiting. What should I do?
In general, it helps to rest and take small sips of water, or an oral rehydration drink, little and often. Try plain food only when you feel ready. Get urgent help if there is blood in the vomit, severe stomach pain, confusion, a stiff neck, or if you cannot keep any fluids down for a day. Take extra care with babies, older people and during pregnancy.

## I have diarrhoea. What should I do?
Rest and keep drinking small, regular sips of water or an oral rehydration drink. Wash your hands often to protect others. Get urgent help if there is blood, severe pain, or signs of dehydration such as very dark urine, dizziness or a very dry mouth. Please see a doctor if it lasts more than two days. Take extra care with babies, older people and during pregnancy.

## I have a fever. What should I do?
Rest, drink plenty of fluids, and wear light clothing. Keep the room comfortably cool. Get urgent help if the fever comes with a stiff neck, confusion, trouble breathing, a rash that does not fade, or severe pain, or if it is in a baby under three months. Please see a doctor if it lasts more than three days.

## I have a headache. What should I do?
Rest in a quiet, dim room, drink water, and try to eat regularly. Get urgent help right away if it is sudden and severe, or comes with confusion, weakness, trouble speaking, a stiff neck, fever, or follows a head injury. Please see a doctor if headaches keep coming back.

## I have a cough or a cold. What should I do?
Rest, drink plenty of warm fluids, and keep fresh air in your room. Get urgent help if you have trouble breathing, chest pain, blue lips, or you are coughing up blood. Please see a doctor if the cough lasts more than two weeks, or if a fever does not settle.

## I have a sore throat. What should I do?
Rest your voice, drink plenty of warm fluids, and avoid smoke. Get urgent help if you have trouble breathing or swallowing. Please see a doctor if it lasts more than a week or comes with a high fever.

## I have a stomach ache. What should I do?
Rest, and take small sips of water. Avoid heavy, spicy or oily food for now. Get urgent help if the pain is severe or sudden, if the belly is hard and swollen, or if you cannot pass stool or gas. Please see a doctor if the pain keeps coming back.

## I feel dizzy. What should I do?
Sit or lie down straight away and drink some water. Get up slowly. Get urgent help if the dizziness comes with chest pain, trouble speaking, weakness, fainting, or a severe headache. Please see a doctor if it keeps happening.

## I have a minor burn. What should I do?
Hold the burn under cool running water for about twenty minutes, then cover it loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth. Do not use ice or put creams on it. Get urgent help if the burn is large, deep, on the face or hands, or caused by chemicals or electricity.

## I have a sprain or muscle pain. What should I do?
Rest the area, hold something cold wrapped in a cloth against it for short periods, and raise it when you can. Get urgent help if you cannot put weight on it, it looks bent out of shape, or it is numb. Please see a doctor if it is not improving after a few days.

## I have a minor cut. What should I do?
Press on it with a clean cloth to stop the bleeding, then wash it gently with clean running water and cover it. Get urgent help if the bleeding does not stop after ten minutes of firm pressure, or the cut is deep or dirty.

## I have a skin rash. What should I do?
Avoid scratching, keep the area clean and dry, and stay away from anything that seems to make it worse. Get urgent help if the rash comes with trouble breathing, swelling of the face or mouth, or a high fever. Please see a doctor if it spreads or lasts more than a few days.
