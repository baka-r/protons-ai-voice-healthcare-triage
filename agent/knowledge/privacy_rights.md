## Is my information kept private?
Yes. Your information is shared only with the staff who need it to care for you.

## Is this call recorded?
This call may be recorded for safety and quality, as you were told at the start.

## Can I see my medical records?
Yes. You can request copies from the medical records office on the first floor with your ID card.

## Can I get a second opinion?
Yes. You are welcome to ask for a second opinion, and reception can help with referrals.
