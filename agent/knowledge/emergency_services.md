## Where is the emergency department?
The emergency department is on the east side of the building, with its own entrance and ambulance bay. It is open twenty four hours a day, every day.

## Do you have an ambulance service?
Yes. Our ambulance can be reached through the emergency number 1122 or the hospital helpline. Ambulances are staffed with trained paramedics.

## What happens when I arrive at the emergency department?
A triage nurse sees every patient first and decides who needs to be seen most urgently, so waiting times depend on how serious each case is.

## What should I bring to the emergency department?
Bring your national ID card and any medicines or reports you have, if you can. Do not delay coming in to collect them.

## Do you treat road accidents and injuries?
Yes. The emergency department has a trauma team for accidents and injuries.

## Is the emergency department the right place for a minor problem?
For minor problems such as a mild cough or cold, the outpatient clinics are better and quicker. For anything that feels serious or sudden, please come to emergency or call 1122.
