## What should I bring to my appointment?
Please bring your national ID card, any previous medical reports, and your insurance card if you have one. Please arrive fifteen minutes early to register.

## Do I need to fast before my visit?
Only if your doctor or clinic has told you to. Please follow the instructions you were given.

## How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment?
You can change or cancel an appointment through this phone assistant, or by calling reception. Please give at least twenty-four hours notice.
