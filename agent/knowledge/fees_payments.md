## What is the consultation fee?
In this demonstration, a General Medicine consultation costs fifteen hundred rupees and a specialist consultation costs twenty five hundred rupees. These figures are made up for the demo.

## Is there a fee for a follow up visit?
A follow up visit within seven days with the same doctor is free in this demonstration.

## Can I pay by card or online?
Yes. You can pay by cash, debit card, credit card or bank transfer at the billing desk.

## Do you give discounts?
Please ask the billing desk about discounts for senior citizens and for patients who qualify for assistance.

## Can I get a receipt or an itemised bill?
Yes. Both are available from the billing desk, and receipts can be emailed.
