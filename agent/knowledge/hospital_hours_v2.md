## What are your OPD timings?
Our outpatient clinics are open Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 5 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Is the emergency department open all day?
Yes. Our emergency department is open twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year. If someone is in danger, call 1122 or come straight to the emergency entrance.

## What are the pharmacy and laboratory timings?
The pharmacy is open from 8 AM to 10 PM every day. The laboratory is open from 8 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday.
