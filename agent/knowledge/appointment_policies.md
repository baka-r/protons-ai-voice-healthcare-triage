## What if I am late for my appointment?
Please arrive fifteen minutes early. If you are more than twenty minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment.

## What is the cancellation policy?
Please cancel at least twenty-four hours before your appointment so that someone else can use the slot.

## Can I bring someone with me to my appointment?
Yes. One companion may come with you. Children must always be accompanied by an adult.

## How long does an appointment take?
Most appointments take between fifteen and thirty minutes, but waiting times can vary.

## Can I get a same-day appointment?
Same-day appointments depend on availability. Please ask me to check, or speak with reception.

## Do I need a referral letter?
Some departments ask for a referral letter. Our reception staff can confirm this for your department.

## How do I collect my laboratory reports?
Laboratory reports can be collected from the laboratory counter on the ground floor with your ID card. Reception can tell you about other options.

## What if I miss my appointment?
If you miss an appointment, please call us to book a new one. Repeated missed appointments may affect how soon we can offer you another slot.
