## Where is the hospital located?
Protons Demo Hospital is on Main Boulevard in Lahore, next to the central park. This is a fictional address for the demo.

## Is there parking available?
Free parking is available behind the main building for the first two hours. Accessible parking spaces are next to the main entrance.

## How do I reach the hospital by public transport?
Bus route twelve stops outside the main gate, and the nearest metro station is a five minute walk away.
