## Do you have a blood bank?
Yes. The blood bank is on the ground floor and accepts donations from nine in the morning to four in the afternoon, Monday to Saturday.

## Do you have physiotherapy?
Yes. The physiotherapy unit is on the first floor next to Orthopedics. Sessions are by appointment.

## Do you have vaccination services?
Child vaccinations are given in the Pediatrics vaccination clinic on Tuesday and Friday afternoons. Adult vaccinations are available at General Medicine.

## Do you offer health check up packages?
Yes. Executive and basic health check up packages are available. Reception or the billing desk can give you the list and prices.

## Is there a pharmacy inside the hospital?
Yes. The pharmacy is on the ground floor and is open from eight in the morning to ten at night every day.

## Can the pharmacy refill my prescription?
The pharmacy supplies medicines against a doctor's prescription. For a repeat prescription, please see your doctor.

## Is there a quiet room or prayer room?
There is a quiet waiting lounge and a prayer room on the first floor.
